## 💡 Using the Shared Inbox

Your team shares one view of the inbox. When someone reads an email, others see the update. This prevents multiple people from answering the same text.

*   **Real-time updates**: Messages arrive instantly. You do not need to refresh your screen.
*   **AI-assisted replies**: Click the AI icon inside an email window. The system suggests a draft based on previous messages.
*   **Team notes**: Post internal notes on the side of any email. Your teammates see these comments immediately.

## 🛡 Security and Privacy

Your data stays protected. All traffic flows through secure servers. You keep control of your mailbox settings at all times. Since the app uses cloud technology, your email history remains safe even if your local computer stops working.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting Issues

If you encounter problems, check these areas:

*   **Network Errors**: Ensure your computer connects to the internet. Restart your router if the app cannot reach the cloud.
*   **Login Issues**: Verify your username and password. Some email providers require an App Password for third-party programs.
*   **Performance**: If the app feels slow, close other programs running in the background. Update your web browser to the latest version.

## 📂 Managing Storage
